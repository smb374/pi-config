## Common rules (fixed)

1. **IDs.** Keep every ID you get. Acceptance criteria are `AC-n`; when a brief groups criteria by ticket, cite a criterion as `#<ticket> AC-n`. Findings are `SPEC-n`, `STD-n`, `COR-n`, or `PLAN-n` by axis. Conflicts are `C-n`. Scan items are `SCAN-n`. When you continue the report of another agent, keep its IDs and continue its numbers.
2. **Status.** The report starts with `status: done` or `status: failed`. `done`: you did the whole brief. `failed`: you stopped before the end, and the report names the cause.
3. **Evidence.** A `search_graph` qualified name also counts as evidence.
4. **Ask.** Ask the parent with `ask_parent`, then end your turn.
5. **Report.** Your final message is the Report section of this brief, filled in. Write nothing before the status line and nothing after the last section.
